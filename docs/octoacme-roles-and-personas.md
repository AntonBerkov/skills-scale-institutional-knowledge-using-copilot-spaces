# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads ensure that deliverables meet defined quality standards and acceptance criteria. They work closely with development teams and product owners to establish quality expectations and validate that they are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and implement quality standards for deliverables
- Conduct QA reviews at key project milestones
- Document defects and coordinate resolution with developers
- Report quality metrics and test coverage to the Project Manager
- Participate in acceptance criteria definition and validation
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality deliverables that meet business requirements
- Minimize defects reaching production
- Build quality culture across the team
- Provide early feedback to reduce rework

### Interactions
- Works with **Developers** on quality criteria and test specifications
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Reports findings and quality metrics to **Project Managers** and **Stakeholders**
- Partners with **Change Management Owner** to assess impact of changes
- Escalates critical issues to **Project Managers**

### Typical Communication
- QA status reports and defect summaries
- Test coverage reports and quality dashboards
- Participation in sprint planning and retrospectives
- Escalation notifications for critical quality issues

---

## Change Management Owner

### Role Summary
Change Management Owners assess the impact of project changes, develop communication strategies, and manage stakeholder expectations around modifications to scope, timeline, or deliverables. They ensure that changes are properly approved, tracked, and communicated.

### Responsibilities
- Assess impact of proposed project changes on timeline, resources, and stakeholders
- Develop change communication strategies and materials
- Track change requests and manage approval workflows
- Manage stakeholder communication around changes and their implications
- Maintain change logs and impact documentation
- Coordinate with Product Managers and Project Managers on change prioritization
- Identify and mitigate risks associated with changes

### Goals
- Minimize scope creep and maintain project control
- Ensure all stakeholders understand change implications
- Reduce rework and project delays caused by uncontrolled changes
- Maintain stakeholder confidence through transparent communication

### Interactions
- Receives change requests from **Project Managers**
- Coordinates with **Product Managers** on change prioritization and business impact
- Communicates changes to **Stakeholders** and affected teams
- Works with **Quality Assurance Lead** to assess quality implications
- Escalates high-impact changes to **Project Managers**
- Collaborates with **Developers** on technical feasibility assessment

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and risk analyses
- Change notifications and stakeholder updates
- Change log maintenance and documentation
- Regular change status reports to Project Managers

---

## Stakeholder Communication Manager

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Communication Managers coordinate regular updates with stakeholders, manage escalation communications, and ensure consistent messaging across all project communications. They serve as the primary communication hub between the project team and external stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate regular stakeholder updates (status reports, demos, briefings)
- Manage escalation communications for critical issues and risks
- Document and track stakeholder feedback and concerns
- Ensure consistent messaging across all communications
- Create and maintain stakeholder communication plans
- Identify and engage stakeholders at appropriate levels
- Manage stakeholder expectations and satisfaction

### Goals
- Maintain transparency and trust with all stakeholders
- Ensure stakeholders receive timely, accurate information
- Minimize surprise escalations and communication gaps
- Support informed stakeholder decision-making

### Interactions
- Reports to **Project Managers** for guidance on communication priorities
- Receives project status information from **Developers**, **Product Managers**, and **Project Managers**
- Engages directly with **Stakeholders** on all project communications
- Coordinates with **Change Management Owner** on change communications
- Partners with **Quality Assurance Lead** on quality-related stakeholder updates
- Escalates major concerns to **Project Managers**

### Typical Communication
- Weekly stakeholder status updates and briefings
- Escalation notifications and incident communications
- Stakeholder feedback summaries and sentiment analysis
- Communication plan documentation

---

## Resource Scheduler

### Role Summary
Resource Schedulers manage resource allocation across projects, track resource availability and capacity, and optimize team utilization. They help resolve resource conflicts and provide forecasting to leadership to support strategic planning.

### Responsibilities
- Manage resource allocation across multiple projects
- Track resource availability, capacity, and utilization
- Identify and resolve resource conflicts across projects
- Optimize team utilization and skill matching
- Provide resource forecasting and capacity planning to leadership
- Support onboarding and skill development planning
- Coordinate with Project Managers on resource needs and timing

### Goals
- Maximize resource utilization and project throughput
- Reduce resource conflicts and scheduling delays
- Support strategic capacity planning
- Enable consistent project delivery

### Interactions
- Works with **Project Managers** on resource needs, timing, and priorities
- Collaborates with team leads (represented by **Developers**) on resource availability and capacity
- Provides resource forecasting and recommendations to **Stakeholders** and leadership
- Coordinates with **Change Management Owner** on resource implications of changes
- Escalates resource conflicts to **Project Managers**

### Typical Communication
- Resource capacity and allocation reports
- Capacity forecast and planning documents
- Resource conflict resolution communications
- Utilization dashboards and metrics
